From Two Places, One Purpose

Anatolia and the Silk Road

We carry two distinct textile traditions — woven rugs and kilims from Türkiye, and hand-embroidered suzani from Uzbekistan. They are constantly muddled together in the resale market. We keep them straight, and we say where a piece is actually from.

Everything is bought in person, one piece at a time, and almost all of it exists exactly once.
