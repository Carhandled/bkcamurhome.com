Who This Is For

A specific vision, met with a specific piece.

Custom sourcing is for interior designers working a client project who need a piece to a size and palette, and for homeowners who already know exactly what they're looking for and haven't found it yet. You tell us what the room needs and we start hunting straight away — working our dealers and contacts in Anatolia and Uzbekistan from the day your brief lands, not holding it for a future buying trip.
